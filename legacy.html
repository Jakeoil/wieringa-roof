Legacy Explorer

Unfold a net by hand. Click any rhomb to seed it, then click rhombs touching the net and each is unfolded across the shared edge. You choose the route; Net does the same search automatically.

Click a rhomb to place it; click an edge to choose the hinge
Clicking an edge picks the hinge. When a rhomb touches the net along more than one edge, which edge you unfold across decides where it lands — clicking the rhomb takes the most recent neighbour, clicking the edge names the one you want. The pointer changes shape over an edge.

Overlaps are allowed and flagged. A placement that lands on top of something already there is still made, but drawn faded with a red dashed outline. That is the whole point: routes that overlap are how the surface's curvature shows itself, and a naive route can overlap a dozen times where a good one never does.

Creases are dashed by fold angle — 36°, 72°, 108° — and coloured red for mountain, blue for valley. Solid black edges are cuts. Click a rhomb on the work canvas to take it off again. Hills up / Dales up flips the roof vertically, reversing every height and every crease. All seven seeds are here, including Deca.
