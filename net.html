Agreed design

Patches
Star, boat and diamond seeds, each expanded and printed as its own one-sheet model. No cross-sheet edge matching.
Decomposition
Greedy maximal ribbon strips — repeatedly take the longest dual-graph path whose consecutive shared edges are parallel. Provably free of overlap, so no overlap test is needed.
Scale
Side s = 20 mm. Strips advance 0.894 s = 17.9 mm per face, so ten faces fit a Letter row.
Annotation
Every crease labelled with its fold angle — 36°, 72° or 108°, never 0° — plus mountain or valley. Cut lines solid, creases dashed by angle.
Output
SVG at exact physical units with a print stylesheet, then the browser's own Save as PDF. No PDF library, and reliable 1:1 scale.
