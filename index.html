The Wieringa Roof

Lift every rhombus of a Penrose tiling out of the plane and it becomes a golden rhombus — one single shape, thirty of which close up into a rhombic triacontahedron. The result is a folded, non-repeating surface of hills and dales whose shadow is the tiling you started with.

This site builds printable nets for that surface. Because every face is the same golden rhombus, a physical model needs only one cut shape; the Penrose tiling supplies nothing but the pattern of which rhombus joins which, and at what angle.

The seeds

Penrose's original tiling is pentagons interspersed with three other shapes — the star, the boat and the diamond. Each is a seed a patch can be grown from, a generation at a time. The three pentagons carry the rhombs:

TileP1 shapeRhombi at gen 1
Pe5pentagon5 thick
Pe3pentagon3 thick + 1 thin
Pe1pentagon1 thick + 2 thin

The star, boat and diamond (St5, St3, St1) seed patches too, though they emit no rhombs until a generation later, and the composite Deca makes seven in all.
