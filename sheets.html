Joins. Each cut carries a tab shaped like the rhombus it joins to — the adjoining rhombus truncated at text height, which comes out a parallelogram because a rhombus's adjacent angles are supplementary. It is lettered with the sheet its other half is on: A▸3 tapes to A▸1 on sheet 3. Every sheet also carries a thumbnail of the whole net with that sheet shaded and every join marked, so you can see which end you are holding.

All sheets share one orientation, so the printed pages lie together the way they assemble. Print at 100% — no scaling, no fit-to-page — and measure a rhombus edge against the side length before cutting anything.

Back side swaps hills for dales, for printing the underside. Reflection does not matter here: every patch has a mirror axis.
