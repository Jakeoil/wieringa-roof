Sheets to Cut

Finished nets at true size, one model per page. Every one is a single connected piece with no overlaps — cut the outline, score the creases, fold. Print at 100% with no scaling and no "fit to page".

Check the scale before you cut anything. Print one page and measure a rhombus edge against the side length in its caption. Browsers and drivers silently rescale; everything else here is exact, so this is the only step that can go wrong.

Creases are dashed by fold angle — 36°, 72°, 108° — and coloured red for mountain, blue for valley. Solid black is a cut. Those numbers are the turn away from flat; the angle between the two finished panels is the supplement, 144°/108°/72°. See Tools for gauges and jigs cut to those angles.
